Pawsition Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Pawsition ("the app") is a puzzle game published by Hakaru Games. The app is suitable for players of all ages, including children.

We collect no personal information

The app does not collect, transmit or share any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. It has no user accounts, no chat, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking or data-collecting SDKs.

Data stored only on your device

Your game progress (current level, coins, boosters, daily streak, practice league score and settings) is saved only on your device so you can continue where you left off. It never leaves the device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or choose "Reset all progress" in Settings.

The in-app "Practice League" is simulated on your device with practice rivals. No information about you is sent to other players or to us.

Permissions

The app does not request any special device permissions and does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children. If we ever add online features, advertising or in-app purchases, we will only use services that comply with Google Play's Families Policy and applicable children's privacy laws (such as COPPA and GDPR-K), and we will update this policy before those features are released.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Hakaru Games using the developer contact details on the app's Google Play page.
