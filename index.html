Pawsition Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Pawsition ("the app") is a puzzle game published by Hakaru Games. The app is suitable for players of all ages.

We collect no personal information

We do not collect, store or share personal information about you, including from children under 13. The app has no user accounts of its own, no chat, no advertising, no analytics and no advertising ID. We do not run servers that receive your data.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (current level, coins, boosters, daily streak, league score, cat outfits and settings) is saved on your device so you can continue where you left off. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or choose "Reset all progress" in Settings.

The in-app League is simulated on your device with computer rivals. No information about you is sent to other players or to us.

Optional cloud save (Google Play Games)

If you choose "Save progress" in Settings, the app signs in to Google Play Games and stores a copy of your game progress in your own Google account using Play Games Saved Games, so you can restore it on another phone. The app reads your Play Games display name only to show which account is linked. This data is stored and processed by Google, not by us; see the Google Privacy Policy. You can stop using cloud save at any time and delete saved game data from the Google Play Games app.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app uses Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver them. Firebase assigns your device a random push token and installation ID, used only to deliver notifications to the topics you chose in Settings (essential notices, and optionally game reminders and news & offers). We do not link these IDs to you, store them on our own servers or use them for advertising. Game reminders (daily puzzle, streak) are scheduled on your device. You can turn notifications off in Settings or in Android's settings. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Firebase processes data.

Deleting your data

To delete your Pawsition data:

  • Game progress on your phone: in Pawsition open Settings and choose "Reset all progress", or uninstall the app.
  • Cloud save: open the Google Play Games app, go to your profile, then Settings, and delete Pawsition's data (or remove it from your Google account's Play Games data at myaccount.google.com). This deletes your saved game progress and Pawsition's Play Games data.
  • Notification IDs: the push token and installation ID are deleted when you uninstall the app.

We keep no copy of any of this data. If you need help, email info@hakaruinfotech.com.

Ratings

After a few levels the app may show Google Play's own rating card. Any rating or review you leave is handled by Google Play; the app is not told whether or how you rated it.

Permissions

The app may ask for permission to show notifications, and it schedules reminders that survive a restart of your phone. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Children

We collect no personal information from children. The only third-party services the app uses are Google services described above (Google Play Games, Firebase Cloud Messaging and Google Play's rating card). If we ever add advertising or in-app purchases, we will only use services that comply with Google Play's Families Policy and applicable children's privacy laws (such as COPPA and GDPR-K), and we will update this policy before those features are released.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Hakaru Games at info@hakaruinfotech.com or via hakaruinfotech.com.
